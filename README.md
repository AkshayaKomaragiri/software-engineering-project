Elicitation:

These three interviews were done at my neighborhood gym, where I asked members of my community for a short interview consisting of three questions. 

Participants will be kept anonymous. 

- #1) What is the biggest problem that they faced in maintaining consistency?
- #2) When you are at the gym, how important is it to you to see your past performance (like what weight you lifted last week) before starting your next set?
- #3)How do you currently track your workouts, weights, and repetitions, and what is the most frustrating part of that process?

* Interview 1:
Middle-aged woman who is balancing work, being a parent, managing family, and still trying to get into working out more frequently. Only started working out 4 months ago. Her workout routine is primarily 3 times a week, with 1-hour sessions.
  - 1.) Not being able to see progress. It's hard to keep up motivation.
  - 2.) Pretty important to her. She has a busy schedule, so she said that she can't get in as many days consecutively. Seeing improvement, however slow, said would give her a boost.
  - 3.) She just keeps them in memory. The frustrating part is that it is easy to forget. 


* Interview 2:
Teenage girl, training for health. Has been working out for 6 months, and her routine is 4 days a week with 1 hour 30 min sessions.
  - 1.) She mentioned burnout from managing school and workout sessions. Sometimes she just feels too lazy.  
  - 2.) said that being able to see it would gamify the experience and might improve her likelihood of showing up.
  - 3.) Uses the Notepad app. The frustrating part is readability. It has just become a lost list.
